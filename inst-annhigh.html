St. Anne’s Girls’ Higher Secondary School, Krishnagiri

Correspondent: Sr. S. Amala Mary

Headmistress: Sr. Magdalena Mary

Total Number of Girl Students: 1,986

# Our School’s History and Growth

Situated in the heart of the beautiful town of Krishnagiri, our school bears the name of St. Anne, the mother of the Blessed Virgin Mary. Standing tall and growing in stature, the school has become a source of pride and admiration.

Inspired by the motto “Love and Service,” the Franciscan Missionaries of Mary (FSM) sisters came to Indian soil from distant lands, dedicating themselves to serving the poor and underprivileged. Like white doves bringing peace and angels spreading kindness, they devoted their lives to uplifting the poor and helping young girls build a brighter future.

Under the dedicated service of the FSM sisters, our school has grown through the following stages:

  • 1937: Established as a Primary School.
  • 1954: Upgraded to a Middle School.
  • 1978: Upgraded to a High School.
  • 1996: Upgraded to a Higher Secondary School.

# A Growing Community of Learners

Our school began with a small number of students, with the aim of nurturing young girls into compassionate and responsible human beings. Today, it proudly serves 1,986 girl students, supported by 57 dedicated teachers, 8 administrative staff members, and 4 staff members responsible for providing midday meals.

The school continues to move forward through the commitment, dedication, and hard work of its entire staff.

# Academic Excellence

From the time the school began presenting students for the public examinations, it has maintained a 100% pass rate every year, according to the school’s records. Students have also secured full marks in several subjects.

The school has achieved notable success in the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS) examination. Through the efforts of the present Headmistress, Sr. Magdalena Mary, 40 students qualified for the NMMS scholarship in the reported year. Eligible students receive ₹1,500 per month for four years, helping them continue their education.

These achievements add further distinction to our school.

# Scouts, Guides, and Social Service

For the past 35 years, the Scouts and Guides movement has played an important role in our school. Built on four essential values — good character, physical well-being, handicraft training, and service to others — the movement helps students develop discipline, responsibility, and leadership.

Every year, around 120 Guides earn the prestigious Rajya Puraskar (Governor’s Award) at the state level, helping them grow into responsible citizens.

The Red Cross movement encourages compassion and a spirit of service, while the Green Corps promotes environmental awareness and the protection of nature. Both initiatives contribute to the all-round development of our students.

Our students also participate in district-level cultural programmes on Independence Day and Republic Day. Their performances have earned recognition and mementoes from the District Collector, bringing pride to the school.

# The Selfless Service of Our Teachers

The school follows the wisdom of the Tamil saint-poet Thiruvalluvar:

*“Having carefully considered what a person can accomplish and by what means, entrust the task to that person.”*

Guided by this principle, our teachers identify each student’s individual abilities and encourage her to develop her talents. As a result, students excel not only in academics but also in sports, arts, science, literature, and other fields.

The school’s former students have reached great heights and are now working in different countries around the world, spreading their knowledge and talents.

Our teachers inspire students with a powerful message:

*“The distance is not too great — if you believe in yourself, you can cross even the sea. The height is not too high — if you are determined, you can climb even a mountain and achieve your goal.”*

Through constant encouragement, the teachers help students become disciplined, confident, clear-thinking women who are ready to shape a better world. They nurture courage, perseverance, and the spirit of leadership in every student.

# A Legacy of Achievement

St. Anne’s Girls’ Higher Secondary School continues to earn recognition for providing quality education in Krishnagiri District. With the blessings of Our Lady of Fatima and the dedicated service of the FSM sisters, teachers, and staff, the school remains committed to academic excellence, moral values, social responsibility, and the empowerment of young women.

We look forward to achieving many more milestones and helping future generations of students grow into capable, compassionate, and responsible citizens.
