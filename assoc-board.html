Parish Board

Every Christian family has a responsibility to participate in the pastoral ministries of the Church. We should actively participate in the life of our parish and diocese. For this purpose, various participatory bodies have been established in the Church.

# Parish Pastoral Council

[Insert image: `assoc-board-1.jpg`]

  • Members of the Parish Pastoral Council should understand the importance of their shared responsibility and work collaboratively with the parish priests.
  • The formation of a new Parish Pastoral Council must take place under the supervision of the Diocesan Pastoral Centre and in accordance with diocesan guidelines.
  • Women's participation should be ensured at all levels, and interested women should be encouraged to take up leadership responsibilities.
  • Members should recognise that the Parish Pastoral Council can help develop the parish in collaboration with the Parish Priest. Therefore, qualified, committed and interested individuals should be selected.
  • Anbiyams and the Parish Pastoral Council should not be approached with a political mindset.
  • The term of office of the Council is three years. Members may be re-elected for the following three-year term. No member should serve for more than two consecutive terms, that is, more than six consecutive years.
  • Repeated selection of members from the same family to the Council should be avoided.
  • The Parish Pastoral Council should meet at least once every two months.

# Parish Finance Committee

[Insert image: `assoc-treasurer.jpg`]

According to Canon 537 of the Code of Canon Law, a finance committee must function in the parish.

The Finance Committee should work collaboratively with the Parish Priest to help improve, maintain and manage the parish's financial resources in constructive and responsible ways.

  • The term of office of the committee members is three years.
  • The Parish Finance Committee should meet at least once every two months.
