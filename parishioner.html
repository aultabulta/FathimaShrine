Parishioner

# Let Us Grow in the Way of the Gospel

Gospel workers should live as examples in society, having the mind and attitude of Christ and maintaining good moral values in their personal lives.

They should work together with the Parish Priest and the parish community.

Trained Gospel workers should actively participate in the life of their own parish and assist in Anbiyam activities, Holy Mass and catechism classes.

# Let Us Renew Ourselves Through the Word of God

The Bible is the presence of Christ in every Christian family. The Word of God should always be a light, a guide and a companion in our family life. Therefore, we should not only give due respect to the Holy Bible but also read and meditate on it every day, both personally and as a family, at a fixed time.

  • Let us make it a habit to bring the Holy Bible to church worship and Anbiyam meetings.
  • Let us ensure that every member of the family has access to the Holy Bible. The installation of the Bible in every home can be celebrated as an Anbiyam activity, and all family members should practise regular daily Bible reading.
  • Let us support the establishment and maintenance of a Bible Bank in the parish and encourage giving Bibles as gifts on special occasions.
  • The Vacation Bible School (VBS) should be planned in such a way that it creates interest in the Bible among children.
  • Regular attendance at Sunday catechism may be encouraged so that children can receive the Sacraments and become eligible for financial assistance from the parish and diocese.

# Let Us Be a Praying Family

The picture of the Sacred Heart of Jesus has an important place in every Christian family. A special place is given to it and due respect is shown. However, we sometimes forget the important message represented by this image.

We proclaim our faith that “Jesus is the head of our family.” If this is truly our faith, then every family should spend some time speaking with Him and developing a relationship with Him. This is what family prayer is all about—it brings families together.

Family prayer helps us experience the love, mercy and care of Jesus. It renews our Christian life and becomes a great source of strength for family life. Therefore, every Christian family should set aside a specific time for family prayer in its daily schedule.

# Let Us Care for the Elderly

Today, the number of old-age homes is increasing because many elderly people are sent there when families feel unable to spend sufficient time with them or care for them. There are also elderly people who willingly choose to live in such homes.

  • Let us not consider them a burden; let us consider them our guides.
  • Let us value them as our advisers.
  • Let us accept their limitations, treat them with affection and care for them like our own children.
  • Let us provide them with suitable food at regular times.
  • Let us create a peaceful environment where they can rest well.
  • Let us never forget: Today’s elderly were yesterday’s parents, and today’s parents will be tomorrow’s elderly.

# Family Handbook

This handbook clearly shows that you are members of this parish. Your names and other details are recorded in it. You should feel proud and happy to be members of this parish. As active and committed members, you should live as witnesses to Christ in your daily lives. You also have certain responsibilities and should live according to them.

  • When approaching the Parish Priest for any parish-related need, it is compulsory to bring the parish membership card. Parish membership cards will be issued only to those who live within the parish boundaries.
  • Every married person should have a separate family membership card.
  • Use of the Family Handbook: Parishioners should always bring this family record book when approaching the Parish Priest for matters such as Baptism, First Holy Communion, Confirmation, Marriage, Anointing of the Sick, Funeral services and other parish-related needs. If it is not brought, the required services may not be able to be completed.

# Let Us Give Our Monthly Contribution Willingly

The monthly contribution helps support the parish in various ways, including:

  • Maintenance of the church
  • Salaries for church staff
  • Purchase of items required for the liturgy
  • Conducting the parish feast in a meaningful manner
  • Helping the poor and needy
  • Conducting catechism and liturgical meetings and training programmes
  • Parish development activities
  • Social and pastoral services
  • Other charitable and service activities

# Let Our Relationship with the Parish Priest Become a Means of Family Growth

The Parish Priest is the leader of the parish community. Therefore, maintaining a good relationship with the Parish Priest is important for every family living in the parish.

The parish community is blessed to have different Parish Priests serving at different times. Each Parish Priest serves according to the circumstances and responsibilities of his ministry. We should accept this as the Bishop’s decision and as part of God’s will.

  • Every family should maintain the basic relationships necessary for the growth and well-being of the parish.
  • Parishioners should not hesitate to express their feelings, suggestions and opinions directly to the Parish Priest.
  • If there are differences of opinion regarding parish development and common welfare programmes, let us share them in the appropriate forum. However, we should never act in a way that hinders the growth of the parish.
  • Let us develop a good relationship with the Parish Priest and always maintain a healthy and cooperative relationship in all parish activities.
